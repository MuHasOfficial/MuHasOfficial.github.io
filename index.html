About Me

My engineering focus centers on the intersection of CAD design, structural analysis, and embedded control.

Throughout my coursework and various research projects, such as designing high-G tungsten payload mounting and vibration resistance for high-altitude balloon experiments with NASA collaborators and developing microcontroller-driven environmental control systems, I have ample experience with rapid prototyping, clear technical documentation, and cross-functional team collaboration.
