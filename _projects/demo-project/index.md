## Project Overview
Designed a functional, multi-component flashlight assembly entirely from scratch using SolidWorks to master complex assembly hierarchies, component clearances, and mechanical fit tolerances.

## Key Engineering Features
- **Parametric Thread Design:** Modeled precise internal and external threads for secure head-to-body enclosure and cap retention.
- **Internal Component Integration:** Engineered custom housings to securely fit battery cells, switch mechanisms, and lens retainers.
- **Exploded View & Documentation:** Generated a fully detailed exploded view and bill of materials (BOM) to map out spatial assembly sequence and part numbers.
