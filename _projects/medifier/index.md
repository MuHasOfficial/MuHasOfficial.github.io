## Project Overview
The goal of the **Medifier** was to provide a quick and seamless method for busy parents to provide medicine to children by integrating a medicine dispenser into a pacifier. The primary mechanism enabling this feature was a duckbill valve that opened when a suction force was applied to the pacifier. The Medifier featured a medicine reservoir that could hold 15 mL of liquid medicine, along with a sleek, lightweight design for easy use.


## Key Engineering Features
- **Duckbill Valve Mechanism:** Designed a suction-activated valve that opens only when suction force is applied to the pacifier, preventing premature spills.
- **Integrated Fluid Reservoir:** Engineered a compact, lightweight 15 mL medicine reservoir combined with a sleek outer housing.
- **Rapid Prototyping & Iteration:** Utilized SolidWorks for precise parametric 3D modeling and executed multiple physical 3D printing iterations to refine the mouthguard and cap fit.
