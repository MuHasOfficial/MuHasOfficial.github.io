### Project Summary
The goal of The Medifier was to provide a quick and seamless method for busy parents to administer liquid medicine to children by integrating a medicine dispenser into a pacifier assembly.

### Key Features & Design
* **Duckbill Valve Mechanism:** Designed a suction-activated valve that opens only when a infant applies suction to the pacifier.
* **Liquid Reservoir:** Designed a compact, lightweight 15 mL fluid reservoir.
* **Rapid Prototyping:** Utilized SolidWorks for parametric 3D modeling and executed multiple 3D printing iterations to refine the mouthguard and cap fit.
