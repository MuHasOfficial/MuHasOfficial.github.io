About Me

Hi, I'm Muhammad Hassan, a Mechanical Engineering student at Drexel University pursuing a minor in mathematics. My work focuses on CAD modeling, mechatronics, and hardware prototyping.

Whether I'm designing high-altitude research payloads for NASA collaborations, building parametric SolidWorks models, or developing microcontroller-based systems using Python and C, I love taking hands-on projects from concept to reality.

Feel free to check out my projects or download my resume to learn more about my background and experience.
